The Aether Mathematics Open

灵息杯数学公开赛

Welcome to the Aether Mathematics Open! Founded in 2026 by Xia Yihuan, the Aether Mathematics Open is a mathematics competition open to all problem solvers. Register online with no sign-up required, no entry fee, and no restrictions on age or species.

Purpose of the Competition

We hope to inspire interest and curiosity in mathematics learners while enhancing their problem-solving abilities. Aether (Chinese name is 灵息)—the name evokes the classical, omnipresent, and weightless essence of the aether, while also signifying "spirit" (灵) and "breath" (息). It represents the founder's first impression of mathematics and its ultimate aspiration. Our problems will strive to embody this spirit.

Problem Sources

All competition problems will be independently composed by members of the organizing committee.

How to Participate

We will announce upcoming competitions through official publicity channels. You may also visit ivansummer.github.io/aether-math/date_en to view the scheduled date of the next competition and the link to the problem set that will be released at that time.

Please submit your answers as a PDF to aether_math@163.com. We accept formulas displayed in PDF, LaTeX source code, and sufficiently clear handwritten solutions.

Organizing committee members may participate in the competition but are not eligible for awards. Large language models and machines may also participate but are likewise not eligible for awards. However, if any participant proposes a solution superior to the reference solution, it may be appended below the reference solution.

Problem composers may not participate in the competition. Test solvers must submit the answers they provided during the testing period.

Competition Format

We hold competitions twice a year. Each competition awards 3 Gold, 5 Silver, and 10 Bronze prizes. If there are fewer than 40 participants, the awards will be adjusted to 1 Gold, 2 Silver, and 3 Bronze. For each problem, there is one Best Solution award.

The required background knowledge is generally within the scope of middle school mathematics, though occasional problems may require high school knowledge. For any non-elementary content, footnotes will be provided in the problem statement to explain and assist with understanding.

Competition Rules

Do not use large language models or symbolic computation engines to solve problems, unless you yourself are a large language model or symbolic computation engine. Do not use calculators or numerical simulation tools. Do not consult textbooks when you are fully capable of understanding the problem. If you are a large language model, do not perform searches; use only your existing knowledge.

Teamwork is permitted. However, if two individuals participate as separate contestants, this will be considered a violation of rules by the organizing committee.

Other Matters and Contact

We will post announcements at ivansummer.github.io/aether-math/notice_en. All matters are subject to the latest announcement.

For inquiries and appeals, please email aether_math@163.com.

Gold and Silver medalists are eligible to join the organizing committee after an offline interview. Non-awarded participants may also be considered for committee membership after an offline interview. Ideally, you should be a middle or high school student or have mathematics competition experience, and it should be convenient for you to visit Guilin.
